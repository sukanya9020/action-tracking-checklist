Purpose
Apply a consistent checklist whenever action items are created, reviewed, updated, or summarized.

Instructions
Identify each action item from the user's request or supplied content. Keep separate commitments as separate rows.
For every item, capture: action, owner, due date, priority, status, source or context, dependencies, and next step.
Mark missing required details clearly. Ask only for details that materially affect ownership, timing, or completion.
Normalize status to Not started, In progress, Blocked, or Completed; normalize priority to High, Medium, or Low.
Check quality before presenting the tracker: every open item must have a clear action verb, one accountable owner, a due date or an explicit TBD, and a next step. Flag overdue items, blocked items, conflicting dates, and duplicate actions.
Present the current tracker in a concise table. Follow it with a short summary of overdue items, blockers, upcoming deadlines, and decisions needed.
When the user provides updates, preserve unchanged fields, record the new information, and clearly identify what changed.
Finish by offering the most useful next action, such as assigning missing owners, resolving blockers, or preparing a status summary.
Parameters
Action items, notes, messages, meeting content, or an existing tracker.
Optional filters such as owner, status, priority, project, or date range.
Optional output preference such as table, concise summary, or export-ready structure.
Output
A validated action tracker with normalized fields, issue flags, a concise status summary, and recommended follow-up actions.
